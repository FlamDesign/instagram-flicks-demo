# Flicks on Instagram: concept demo

Clickable concept showing Flicks in five Instagram placements: Reels ads (Zara, Acer, both 9:16; a Flick is the first reel), Stories ads (Coach, Cadillac, Nike), in-feed sponsored posts (Gap, Mercedes-Benz, LEGO), Explore tiles (Levi's, Bentley, Bose at Best Buy) and brand messages (Nike, Airbnb, BARK). The sponsored stories also sit in the stories tray, marked Sponsored. Open "Ad formats" to jump to each one.

In the Reels ads the demo moves each Flick's own thumbnails up, because Instagram draws its caption, call to action and action icons over the bottom of a Reels ad. The demo hides every Flick's own button; where there is a call to action, Instagram supplies it. The creatives themselves are unchanged.

All accounts other than the advertisers, posts and counts are invented. Photos are from Unsplash. Built by Flam for partner conversations. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Instagram, Meta or the advertisers shown.
