# Flicks on Instagram: concept demo

Clickable concept showing Flicks in three Instagram placements: Reels ads (Zara, Acer, both 9:16), Stories ads (Coach, Cadillac, Nike) and in-feed sponsored posts (Gap, Sony, American Express). The sponsored stories also sit in the stories tray, marked Sponsored. Open "Ad formats" to jump to each one.

In the Reels ads the demo moves each Flick's own thumbnails up, because Instagram draws its caption, call to action and action icons over the bottom of a Reels ad. In every placement the demo also hides the Flick's own button, since Instagram supplies the call to action. The creatives themselves are unchanged.

All accounts other than the advertisers, posts and counts are invented. Photos are from Unsplash. Built by Flam for partner conversations. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Instagram, Meta or the advertisers shown.
